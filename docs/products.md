# Products

A company can hold any number of products. Each product is a full workspace:
helpers, skills, tasks, comments, routines, goals, marketing, Ads Studio,
Advise Me, files, financials, integrations and team access.

## Create

Settings → Company → **Products** → name, website, audience, description →
**Add product**.

The product is created under the current company. The parent company's team is
copied onto it, and future team changes are copied as well.

## Work in a product

Open the product from Settings → Products, or from the company switcher in the
sidebar. The header shows a **Product** badge, and every screen is scoped to it.

## Rules

- Products cannot contain other products.
- The parent company cannot be deleted until its products are deleted.
- A product owner can delete the product from Settings → Products.
- Existing company data stays on the parent company. It is the first workspace.

## Why child workspaces

Crew already scopes every feature by company. A product is a child company
internally, so it inherits isolation, budgets, roles and data export without
duplicating the entire feature set. The UI exposes it as a product, not a
separate company.
